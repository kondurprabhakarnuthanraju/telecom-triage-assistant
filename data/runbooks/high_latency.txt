# Runbook: High Latency

## Symptoms
- Response time > 100ms
- Packet loss > 1%
- Jitter > 50ms
- VoIP quality degradation

## Immediate Actions (Priority 2)
1. Check interface utilization on path
2. Verify no routing loops
3. Check for broadcast storms
4. Verify QoS policies are applied

## Escalation Criteria
- Latency persists > 10 minutes
- Customer impact confirmed
- Multiple customers affected

## Severity
- Internal network: Medium
- Customer-facing: High
