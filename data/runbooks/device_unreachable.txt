# Runbook: Device Unreachable

## Symptoms
- Ping/ICMP timeout
- SNMP polling fails
- SSH/management access denied
- Device appears offline in NMS

## Immediate Actions (Priority 1)
1. Check device power status
2. Verify management IP is correct
3. Check routing to device subnet
4. Verify no ACL blocking ICMP/SNMP

## Escalation Criteria
- Device unreachable > 3 minutes
- Multiple devices in same rack/pop
- Device is a core router/switch

## Severity
- Access switch: Medium
- Distribution switch: High
- Core router: Critical
